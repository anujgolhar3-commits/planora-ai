Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Introduction

Planora AI ("we", "our", or "the Service") provides automated circadian schedule intelligence and calendar orchestration. We are committed to transparency and safeguarding your personal information.

2. Google User Data We Collect

When you authenticate via Google OAuth, Planora AI requests permission to access the following data strictly to fulfill user-requested scheduling functionality:

  • Google Profile & Email: To create and authenticate your account workspace session.
  • Google Calendar Events (Read & Write): To detect your existing schedule commitments, identify open deep-work focus windows, and place AI-recommended task blocks onto your calendar with your permission.
Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Planora AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or calendar contents to advertisers or third parties.
  • No Model Training: Your Google Calendar events and personal schedules are never used to train generalized AI foundation models.
  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All communications between your browser, our servers, and Google APIs use TLS 1.3 encryption. Sessions use cryptographically signed authentication cookies.

4. Data Retention and Revocation

You can revoke Planora AI's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions. Logging out immediately invalidates your session.
